# Ridgeline Capital — Investment Mandate

> **Note:** Ridgeline Capital is a **fictional** private equity fund created for this portfolio project. The strategy and thresholds below are illustrative assumptions, not investment advice. All company data used in the screen comes from public SEC filings.

## 1. Fund Overview

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Fund size | $2.0 billion |
| Number of investments | 6–10 platform companies |
| Equity per deal | ~$200–330 million |
| Typical structure | ~50% equity / ~50% debt |
| Target purchase price (EV) | ~$400–800 million |
| Holding period | 3–7 years |
| Deal type | Control buyouts; screening U.S.-listed companies as take-private candidates |

## 2. Strategy: U.S. B2B Business Services

Ridgeline targets companies that provide outsourced, specialized services to other businesses (e.g., testing and inspection, facility services, IT managed services, compliance).

**Why this sector:**
- **Asset-light:** low capital spending means most EBITDA converts to free cash flow, which can repay acquisition debt.
- **Predictable revenue:** multi-year contracts and repeat customers support stable cash flows that lenders value.
- **Fragmented markets:** many small competitors create add-on acquisition opportunities ("buy-and-build").

**Excluded:** staffing and PEO businesses (structurally thin margins), financial services, real estate, companies with negative EBITDA, and companies already subject to an announced acquisition.

## 3. Size Gates (must pass to be considered)

| Metric | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Revenue | $250 million – $1 billion |
| EBITDA | $40 – 100 million (must be positive) |
| Geography | U.S.-listed, U.S.-headquartered |

## 4. Core Screening Metrics (provisional thresholds)

| Metric | Formula | Preferred range | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue growth (3-yr CAGR) | (Rev_t / Rev_t-3)^(1/3) − 1 | 5–10% | Steady demand supports EBITDA growth |
| EBITDA margin | EBITDA / Revenue | ≥ 12% | Pricing power and efficiency |
| FCF conversion | (CFO − CapEx) / EBITDA | ≥ 70% | Cash available to repay debt |
| CapEx intensity | CapEx / Revenue | < 3% | Confirms asset-light model |
| Net leverage | (Total debt − Cash) / EBITDA | < 3.0x | Room for acquisition debt |
| ROIC | NOPAT / Avg. invested capital | Set after reviewing data | Business quality |
| EV / EBITDA (current) | EV / EBITDA | ≤ ~10x preferred; > ~13x likely too expensive | Leaves room for a 20–40% control premium |

## 5. Diagnostic Flags (reviewed, not scored)

- **EBITDA margin stability:** 3-year margin swing above ~5 percentage points
- **Days sales outstanding (DSO):** high or rising versus peers
- **Acquisition spending / revenue:** significant reliance on purchased growth

## 6. Qualitative Review (finalists only)

Recurring/contracted revenue, customer concentration (customers >10% of revenue), labor cost exposure, and end-market cyclicality. Sourced from 10-K text and verified against the filing.

## 7. Methodology Conventions

- **EBITDA** = reported operating income + depreciation & amortization (not company-defined "Adjusted EBITDA")
- **Debt** excludes operating lease liabilities
- **Tax rate** for NOPAT: 25% assumption applied uniformly
- **Period:** most recent fiscal year from annual 10-K filings; share prices recorded with an "as of" date
- **Data source:** SEC EDGAR (XBRL financial data); market prices from a documented source

## 8. Known Limitations

- Public companies are used as proxies; most real middle-market PE targets are private.
- Annual figures are used rather than trailing-twelve-month figures, creating a timing gap with current share prices.
- Thresholds are provisional and may be revised after reviewing real data; any revision will be documented.
